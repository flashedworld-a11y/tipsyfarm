Tipsy Farm – Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026

The short version

Tipsy Farm has no accounts and never asks for your name or email. Your progress stays on your device. The game is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob, which may collect some device information, explained below.

What is stored on your device

Your coins, the fields you have finished and your best times, the items you own, your chosen squad and theme, your ad counters and your settings (sound, haptics, dark mode). This is saved locally in the app. We never receive it.

Motion sensors

The game reads your phone's tilt sensor to move the field. The readings are used on the spot, only while you play, and are never stored or sent anywhere.

Ads

The game shows a full-screen ad from time to time, and offers optional videos you can watch for bonus coins. To show and measure ads, Google AdMob and its partners may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, device type and how you interact with ads. Depending on where you live, you are asked for your choice before ads are personalised, and on iPhone the system asks permission before the advertising identifier is used. You can change your choice at any time in Settings under Ad privacy choices, or in your iPhone settings. Learn more in Google's policy at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, precise location, contacts or photos, and we do not run any analytics tools of our own. We do not sell your data.

Third parties

Two third-party services are built into the app. Google AdMob shows the ads (see Ads above). RevenueCat helps us confirm and restore your purchases: it receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase records, and it may also see technical details such as your IP address and device type. We never give either service your name or email, because we do not have them. You can read their policies at policies.google.com/technologies/ads and revenuecat.com/privacy.

Purchases

The game offers optional in-app purchases. Apple processes your payment, and we never see or store your payment details. RevenueCat (see Third parties) helps us deliver and restore what you buy.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices and deleting your data

You can erase all saved progress at any time in Settings, using Erase all progress. Deleting the app removes the saved data from your device. We hold no copy of your progress on a server. To limit ad tracking, use Ad privacy choices or Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking on your iPhone.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date below and show the new version in the app.

Contact
